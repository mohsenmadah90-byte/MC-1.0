# Charger Particle Design — Phase 1.2

## States

- `mcity:charger_powered`: blue-white upward induction, low/medium rate.
- `mcity:charger_charging`: green upward energy, higher rate.
- `mcity:charger_spark`: short bright electrical spark for state transitions/pulses.

All effects use the Vanilla particle atlas and are ready for a later Block State/Animation Controller integration. No charge or gameplay behavior was changed in this asset phase.
